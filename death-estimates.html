U.S. ESTIMATES · 2024

Cancer deaths by age group.

The National Cancer Institute reports estimated cancer deaths for children and adolescents in two broad age groups. These are annual population estimates, not observed counts or an individual child's risk.

How to read thisNCI describes these as estimates for 2024, not final registry counts. Differences between the two age groups do not explain why outcomes differ and should not be used to predict a particular child's prognosis. Ask a pediatric oncology team about individual questions.

Source and definitions

National Cancer Institute, “Cancer in Children and Adolescents.” The source reports 1,590 estimated deaths among ages 0–19, including 1,040 among ages 0–14 and 550 among ages 15–19 in 2024.
