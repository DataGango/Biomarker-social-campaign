Caption
Everyone deserves dignity, safety and support. Exploitation is never the survivor's fault.

Human trafficking can involve force, fraud or coercion to exploit people for labor or commercial sex. It can affect people of any age or background. This is general awareness information, not a checklist for identifying a person as trafficked.

A safer response: listen without judgment, respect privacy and seek trained help when it is safe. Do not confront suspected traffickers, investigate or attempt a rescue yourself. Do not post names, photos, locations or identifying details about someone who may be at risk.

United States only: the National Human Trafficking Hotline offers trained support at 1-888-373-7888, 24/7. Outside the U.S., seek a trusted local specialist support service. In immediate danger, contact local emergency services if safe; in the U.S., call 911.

If someone may be monitoring your device, consider whether contacting support from it is safe. A trained advocate can help discuss safety options tailored to your situation.
