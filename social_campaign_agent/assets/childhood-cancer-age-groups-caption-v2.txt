One number can’t tell every story. In the United States, NCI estimated that 14,910 people ages 0–19 would be diagnosed with cancer in 2024: 9,620 children ages 0–14 and 5,290 adolescents ages 15–19. These are population estimates, not a prediction for any individual child.

Bring questions about a diagnosis, tests, treatment options, and follow-up to the pediatric oncology team.
